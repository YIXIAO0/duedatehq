Quick read

All 8 above are free to use. Same content, just type. Differences are subtle in chips/labels and louder in the hero.

A · Inter — safest. Boring? Notion / Linear / GitHub all chose it. Dify too.
B · Geist — current. Dev-tool feel; rounder than Inter at heavy weights.
C · General Sans — refined humanist. "Design studio" energy without being precious.
D · Switzer — distinctive grotesque. More personality on letters like 'a' and 'g'. European product vibe.
E · Satoshi — slightly geometric, premium. Closest free cousin of Söhne (Anthropic / Stripe Press).
F · Plus Jakarta Sans — friendly. Pairs with Arc's playful colors well; reads warm.
G · Cabinet × Inter — display + body mix. Hero is editorial / retro; body stays neutral.
H · Inter + serif italic — most "designed". Use the italic on one word, not a sentence. High taste, low risk if restrained.

My read: for an Arc-DNA product with per-client color and warm gradients, E · Satoshi or C · General Sans read most "tasteful" — both have refined letter shapes that elevate the type without distracting from the data. F · Plus Jakarta is the warmest match for the playful color system. Avoid Switzer if you want to stay calm — its character is louder. Geist (current) is fine but tilts engineering-ish.
