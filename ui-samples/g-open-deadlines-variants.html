Open deadlines — 4 layout variants

Same client (9 deadlines, 6 different dates) rendered four ways. All variants share the SF Lightning palette so the comparison is structural, not chromatic.

1 · Polished bands (refined version of what you have now)

Keeps the structure you already have. Refinements: full-width date band reads as a real header, urgency now expressed as a colored left stripe down the entire date group (band + rows), bigger chevrons, subtle row dividers.

2 · Date pills + breathing cards

Each date is a free-floating pill at the top-left of its own card. More vertical breathing space between dates. Reads less as "table" and more as "this is what's happening on each day". Heavier visual but easier to skim long-horizon lists.

3 · Timeline rail

A continuous vertical rail on the left with date nodes. Stronger sense of time flowing forward. Less "tabular", more "calendar agenda". Good for the CPA who plans by time, not by form.

4 · Compact pro-tool

Densest of the four. Form code in a fixed-width left column, jurisdiction & title in the next, urgency chip on the right. Date is just a quiet thin separator. Closer to Drake / desktop tax software — for power-users who scan many rows fast.

Pick by mental model:

1 — "I want a slightly nicer version of what I have." Lowest risk, smallest jump.

2 — "Each due date is a thing in itself." Best for clients with many spread-out dates.

3 — "I plan by time, not by form." Best storytelling of urgency-as-distance.

4 — "I want to scan 30 rows fast." Best for advisors with high deadline volume per client.
