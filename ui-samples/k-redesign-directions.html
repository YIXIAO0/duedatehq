Quick compare

Same dashboard, six personalities. Each one shows the calendar, smart filters, clients list, agenda buckets, status bars, badges, and announcement strip — so you can judge the whole system, not just hero copy.

A · Linear — hairline + mono + ⌘K. Bucket band → 3px left rail. Fastest to scan.
B · Claude — cream + serif + italic numerics. Buckets become serif sections. Date subheaders italic.
C · Notion — toggle blocks + emoji + callouts. Calendar quiet, no KPI chrome. Best at "feeling like a doc".
D · Stripe — gradient hero + soft shadow cards + big rounding. Bucket bands become gentle background tints.
E · Luma — dark canvas, glow rings, gradient text. Looks great at night. Probably not for 10-hour CPA days.
F · Arc — color-coded clients (avatar + chip + cal dot), rounded-everything. Most distinctive in the CPA market.

My read after rendering everything: B · Claude still wins as the daily skin (calm, distinctive, hard to clone), with A · Linear's row-density tricks borrowed for the agenda area when CPAs zoom in on a heavy week. F · Arc's per-client color is genuinely useful for this product — when 5 clients all have a deadline on Apr 15, color-coding turns "5 dots" into "I can see which clients pile up" — strong candidate to borrow into B. D · Stripe remains the safest "obviously-beautiful" option. Pick one and I'll start rewriting globals.css + the layout shell first, then the dashboard.
